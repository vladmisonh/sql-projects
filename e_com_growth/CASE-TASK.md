# Case Task — SQL + Data Analytics Challenge

## Tasks

### Part A — SQL Analysis

Write SQL queries to answer the following:

1. Monthly revenue and gross margin % trend
2. Top 5 product categories by sales in 2025 YTD
3. 30-day repeat purchase rate (customer retention)
4. RFM segmentation using SQL
5. Revenue by region + performance ranking
6. Coupon usage impact on average order value
7. 60-day cohort conversion rate
8. Categories with >20% revenue drop in May 2025
9. Marketing ROI: ROAS by channel and month
10. Customer Lifetime Value (LTV) estimation (12-month window)

---

### Part B — Business Analytics & Insights

Using SQL findings:
- Explain **what caused the revenue drop in May 2025**
- Identify **which segments were impacted most** (products, regions, traffic source)
- Analyze **marketing ROI and budget efficiency**
- Describe **customer lifecycle and retention trends**

---

### Part C — Recommendations

Provide **3 data-driven business recommendations** and estimate their **expected impact**.
